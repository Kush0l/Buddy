# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-02

First release. A little pet lives in your system tray and reminds you to drink water and eat, and only gets its own water and food when *you* do.

### Pick your reminder friend
- **Buddy** peeks out from the edge of your screen, then hops in with its bowl. Looks: Beagle, Shih Tzu, Husky and Pug (dogs); Orange Tabby, Black Cat and Siamese (cats).
- **Ghosty** floats back and forth across the bottom of your screen, and pauses when your mouse is on it.
- Rename your pets in Settings.

### Glanceable reminders
- A small bubble you understand without reading: 💧 **Water time!** or 🍽️ **Lunch time!**
- Three buttons: **✓** I did it · **⏰ 10m** later · **🔕** no more reminders today.

### The pet game
- Each pet has its own water and food bars that slowly drain while you're at the computer, and only fill up when you drink or eat.
- ✓: your pet eats or drinks with you. ⏰: it gets sad and won't drink either. 🔕: Buddy gives you puppy eyes; Ghosty gets angry.
- A thought cloud shows what your pet wants, with a red **!** when it's running low.
- The tray menu shows your pet's mood and today's drinks and meals.

### Stays out of your way
- **Busy mode** for 30 min, 1 h, 2 h or until tomorrow morning; it always switches back to Normal in the morning.
- **Automatic meeting pause**: no reminders while an app is using your microphone or camera (Teams, Google Meet, Zoom, …), or while you're sharing your screen or showing a slide show.
- Never steals focus, and clicks pass through the empty parts of its window.

### Known limitations
- Windows only for now; a macOS build is planned.
- Mic and camera meeting detection is Windows-only.
- Screen-share detection is confirmed for Chrome and Edge (Meet, Teams or Zoom in the browser); the Teams and Zoom desktop apps' sharing toolbars aren't confirmed yet.
- Calls with mic and camera off and no screen share aren't detected; use Busy mode for those.
- The installers aren't code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (**More info → Run anyway**).
